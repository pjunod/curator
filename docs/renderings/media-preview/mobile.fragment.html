Synopsis

When a night train stops outside an isolated mountain town, a cartographer discovers that every passenger is carrying a different map of the same place. With the tracks buried under fresh snow, she joins a local guide to find a route home.

As they retrace a journey her mother made thirty years earlier, familiar landmarks begin to contradict the stories she grew up with. Each passenger has a reason to keep moving, and someone on the train knows why their destination has vanished from every timetable.
